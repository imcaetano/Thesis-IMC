# About This Repository

My name is Isabela Marques Caetano, and I am a PhD candidate in International Relations at the Institute of International Relations, University of São Paulo (IRI-USP), specializing in International Political Economy. My doctoral research is supervised by Professor Natalia Dus Poiatti Lomonaco, PhD.
I am the author of the doctoral dissertation "The Capital Flows and Green Narratives of the Brazilian-Chinese Strategic Partnership", which seeks to answer the following research question:
Is there a triangular correlation between Chinese investments in Brazil’s energy and green sectors, Brazilian industrial upgrading, and Brazil-China narrative convergence regarding environmental governance?
As a way to address this question, I employ quantitative discourse analysis to develop a Discursive Convergence Index (DCI), which will serve as a key measure of discursive convergence between Brazil and China.

## Tools
### Remove Paragraph Breaks
The first step in this process is to build the textual database. To do so, I developed and used the Remove Paragraph Breaks tool to standardize the speeches collected from official government websites before incorporating them into the original corpus dataset.
